# Competitive and technical research

Checked October 29, 2026. This is a primary-source review of public product descriptions, specifications, and papers. It is not a hands-on competitor evaluation, a market-size study, or evidence that undocumented competitor behavior is absent.

## What the research changes

Finding credits, matching invoices, negotiating renewals, citing sources, tracking amendments, and placing approval checks around agents are already crowded capabilities. Truthful should compete through a concrete, executable assurance workflow and transparent failure behavior. A graph or a hash does not establish differentiation by itself.

## Research and engineering foundations

The recent arXiv items below were opened directly and their titles, authors, dates, abstracts, and available HTML were checked. They are preprints; their claims have not been independently reproduced in this project.

| Source | Verified contribution relevant here | Consequence for our implementation and claims |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ciliberto, Rudi, Tassi, *Proof-Carrying Contracts*, submitted October 20, 2026, arXiv **2610.28484**. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28484), [full HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.28484v1). | Proposes proof-checked operation gating and states assumptions connecting model constraints to world safety. | A JavaScript policy guard is not a machine-checked proof system. Use explicit preconditions and reproducible checks; do not call our packet a proof-carrying contract or claim formal safety. |
| Sharma, *When Is a Reconciliation Break Real?*, submitted October 10, 2026, arXiv **2610.14470**. An `asOf` slider alone does not establish stream completeness. Declare source horizons or label uncertainty. Do not transfer the paper’s measured results to our scenarios. |
| Cai, *Making Duplicate Reimbursement Unrepresentable*, submitted September 29, 2026, arXiv **2609.37819**. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37819). | Presents a guarded invoice lifecycle and reports SMT-checked uniqueness/authorization invariants for an Ethereum design. | Conservation across obligations is established territory. | Yao et al., *τ-bench*, 2024. | Debenedetti et al., *AgentDojo*, 2024. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.13352). | Provides an environment for prompt-injection attacks and defenses around tool-using agents. | Include hostile source instructions and inspect state/effects. | W3C PROV-DM. | Fowler, *Bitemporal History*. [Author’s article](https://martinfowler.com/articles/bitemporal-history.html). | Explains the distinction between when a fact applied and when it became known. | Preserve effective time and observed/recorded time. A late correction should change current understanding without rewriting what an earlier operator could know. |

## V4 mechanism beyond the foundation

The implemented expansion adds a bounded full-history interleaving explorer, shortest effect witness and current-guard replay, fixed-family repair with separate legitimate-completion accounting, and real Ed25519 delegated local execution with pinned-root effect verification. The reference is a deliberately broken cached-check harness, not a competitor. Custom programs can be tested but do not receive general synthesized repair. The public signing lab is an isolated ephemeral fixture; optional SQLite mode is durable and atomic. See [dated frontier research](FRONTIER_RESEARCH.md) for the October 20 Visa pilot, October 24 permission/receipt Internet-Draft, current protocol versions, relevant counterexample/authority research, and falsifiers. None establishes that competitors lack equivalent mechanisms or that Truthful is first.

## Defensible positioning

“Truthful tests whether a proposed financial action is still valid under the current evidence, obligation version, available credit, and delegated authority—and shows why.”

This is an implemented prototype wedge, not a claim of unique intellectual property. It includes adverse reducer cases, conserved totals and historical views, stale-action checks, and a replayable local service record. A finance operator must still find the decisions understandable and useful; no public research establishes customer demand for this exact combination.
