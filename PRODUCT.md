## Platform
web

## Stack
React 19 and Vite 7; plain CSS; browser-local deterministic evidence processing, Web Crypto audit hashes, and localStorage persistence. Node 24 CLI uses the same evidence engine. No API keys required for this build.

## Users
Finance operators following vendor concessions through to invoice application; Ramp hackathon engineers reviewing a working technical build.

## Product Purpose
Connect a vendor promise to evidence of fulfillment. Preserve original terms and authority, calculate remaining value in integer cents, and make each financial result inspectable.

## Operating Context

Deadline November 10, 2026 at 11:59 PM PT; final November 19. The accepted invitation names the team but does not establish its original track. Keep the existing product name Truthful unless the user changes it.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Evidence lab accepts supported promise text, positive applied-credit ledger CSV, and optional vendor reply. Exact vendor, USD, commitment reference and date checks; explicit provenance verification; duplicate handling; source quote offsets; review instead of unsupported claims. Future/originally conditional promises and unknown replies cannot produce an approved recovery draft. A later conditional reply does not amend the original promise. Up to thirty browser-local case snapshots; exact-draft approval; unsent EML, CSV and JSON evidence exports. Hash-linked records establish internal consistency, not external authenticity.

The separate guided story uses fictional Nimbus Cloud and Pipeline CRM source material. Covered nine-hour outage in a 30-day month yields98.75% availability,25% of$8,000=$2,000. Pipeline concession is$2,400 promised,$900 applied,$1,500 outstanding. Issued and applied credits remain separate. Simulated invoice arrivals and replies are explicitly labeled.

## Brand Commitments
Truthful is the product; OVO is the team. Sage navigation, paper work surfaces, forest actions, restrained amber for conditions, Manrope type and Lucide icons. Concise product copy, inspectable evidence, clear approvals. No invented customers, recovery outcomes, integrations or runtime-AI claims.

## Product Principles
- Evidence before assertion; unknown values stay unknown.
- A promise, approval, issued credit and applied credit are distinct.
- Exact references outrank matching amounts.
- Conditions and authority survive the handoff.
- Deterministic financial rules remain authoritative if model extraction is added.
