Fulfilled cases lead to inspection/history rather than another claim proposal.

A multi-case financial workspace conserves credit capacity globally. Issued, applied, reversed and conditional value remain separate. ## Product Principles

- Exact references outrank matching amounts.

- Deterministic financial rules remain authoritative if model extraction is added.
