# Apple and Ramp principles applied to Truthful

This is an interpretation for Truthful’s finance workflow, not affiliation with either company or a copy of their visual identity.

[Apple’s design foundations](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/359/) emphasizes clear structure and navigation, meaningful content hierarchy and progressive disclosure. [Ramp’s design perspective](https://ramp.com/blog/midday-joining-ramp) describes financial tools that require less effort and less time in the product.

The native design applies those ideas in four concrete ways:

- **Start with the finance job.** The main prototype entry is the commitment portfolio; evidence intake and execution analysis remain available as separate entries.
- **Give the decision hierarchy.** The outstanding credit leads the selected case; original promise and applied value remain visible supporting facts. Compact vendor rows make the queue scannable.
- **Show the current context.** Mobile approval names the selected vendor and exact commitment reference. It does not pretend that a static one-case frame has a working multi-case picker.
- **Reveal detail at the right stage.** Approval, scope, receipt and pinned-key verification remain distinct review states. A signed packet is checked on the dedicated verification screen; a successful receipt is not presented as applied savings.

Neutral surfaces, Public Sans, consistent controls, named financial states and adjacent evidence carry the existing working-ledger identity. The design preserves the original controls, modeled trust limits and synthetic-data boundaries.
