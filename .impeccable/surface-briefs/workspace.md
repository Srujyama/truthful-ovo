# Truthful workspace

Mode: Operate. Code-led implementation, user authorized proceeding. No generated raster assets needed. Visual authority is this new build.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A finance incident review desk makes a promise, evidence, and actual fulfillment readable together. Evidence and a clear next action lead the screen.

OWN-WORLD: Chalk-white work surface, pale sage navigation, deep forest action ink, mineral-gray rules, restrained amber for unresolved conditions. Manrope with tabular amounts. Fine dividers and 12px corners; Lucide icons at consistent stroke.

STORY: A user replays an outage, sees a credit calculated from evidence, approves a claim, catches a conditional offer, and verifies application. Selecting the CRM case shows the mechanism working on email concessions.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 210px navigation rail; contextual top bar; 30px task title; three quiet summary figures; a 290px promise register beside the active case. Evidence opens in a side drawer. Recording controls stay at the workspace bottom.

FORM: Engineering incident review, inherited from the existing implementation. The historical seed had no corroborating roll record; this extension makes no concept-roll claim. Other grounded candidates: audit workpaper, flight operations desk, investment report, ledger index, shipment tracker, warranty case folder. Specimen challenger contributes measurement typography; consumer app contributes comfortable targets; reference-setting contributes explicit citations. Mascot, raked timetable, and map systems lose finance clarity. Predictable composition while state changes.

SIGNATURE: Replaying an incident advances a short evidence-matching sequence, reveals the deterministic calculation, and changes claim state. Verified application earns the only celebratory motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
